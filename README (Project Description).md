# Fresh_Carwash

Fresh Car Wash is a company that operates as a reliable car wash and sanitation service provider in Malaysia. Since its establishment in 2020, this innovative company has provided top-notch quality cleaning and detailing service at an affordable price. Located in Taman Maluri, Kuala Lumpur, Fresh Car Wash provides various choices of services such as 
standard cleaning, premium cleaning, vacuum cleaning and full cleaning. As the car wash business is blooming with success, the number of customers the service gained are significantly increasing, causing the service business to expand even further.

However, despite the growing success and increasing popularity of Fresh Car Wash, this has caused several problems and challenges for the employees. First and foremost, it is burdensome for the employees to record each customer's details as the company only uses a record book. This includes the customer’s vehicle type, customer's service type they order, the total price of the service and if the customer has a membership or not. The time to search for specific customer details is also too long because the employees need to flip the book record pages and search for it manually. The additional problem with using a book record page is you have to keep track of current and previous customers since it is valuable data to the company. Hence, a lot of spaces need to be designated specifically for the book records which will be costly for the company.

Moreover, the manual calculation of each customer's payment also adds to the workload of the employees which also proves burdensome considering the technological advancements that are currently available which makes manual processes unnecessary. Although the calculation is quite straightforward, the repetition of the task increases the chances and likelihood of errors. These manual processes not only consume valuable time and energy but also introduce the business to the risks of human errors in payment calculations that may potentially lead to inconsistencies and customer’s dissatisfaction.

To solve these problems, Fresh Car Wash needs to establish a system where the customer details are recorded efficiently. By implementing a modern and technological approach to data management, the company is able to optimize its use of time and energy resources. This transition will ultimately transform the operations of Fresh Car Wash from manual record-keeping to an automated processing digital system. Thus , this new system would not only enhance operational efficiency but also make Fresh Car Wash as a popular choice for customers seeking a reliable car wash and sanitation service provider in Malaysia.
